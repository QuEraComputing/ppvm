Expand description

Storage for φ over the active axes 0..k. Only three things happen to it: a Pauli rotation, an activation, and a collapse; every other change of basis is trivial on the state. It sees axes only, never the tableau.

P is unsigned: the caller folds signs into the coefficients and the outcome. Weights are unnormalized, so rotate need not be unitary.

fn branch_weights(&self, p: AxisPauli) -> [f64; 2]

Unnormalized weights ‖½(1 + P) φ‖² and ‖½(1 − P) φ‖² of the outcomes +1 and −1.

Source

fn collapse(&mut self, p: AxisPauli, m: bool, v: &RotationCollapse)

Project onto outcome m of p (eigenvalue (−1)^m), apply V† for v = RotationCollapse::collapse(p, m), drop axis v.pivot (now |0⟩), and renormalize. Axes above the pivot shift down by one. The branch must have nonzero weight.
